The Basics

What Is a Loan Signing Agent?

A loan signing agent is a notary who meets borrowers to guide them through signing their mortgage documents, verifies identity, completes the notarizations, and returns the package to the title or escrow company. Loan signing agents do not give legal or loan advice or explain loan terms; questions about your loan go to your lender or title company.
