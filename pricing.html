What Your Signing Fee Covers

Each signing fee covers: document review before the appointment (15–30 min), drive to location (15–60 min), the signing appointment itself (45–90 min), and drop-off at FedEx/UPS plus scan-back if required (15–30 min). Total time per signing is typically 2–3.5 hours door to door.

Note on rates: Rates shown are standard market rates for the Scottsdale/Phoenix metro area. Final fee is confirmed at booking.
