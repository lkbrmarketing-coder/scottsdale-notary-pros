Our Notary Services

Professional notarization for every document type — at your location.

Personal & Legal

Wills, POAs, Affidavits & More

We notarize all personal and legal documents with care and professionalism. Whether you're finalizing an estate plan, granting power of attorney, or signing an affidavit, we ensure every document is legally executed and properly certified.

  • Power of Attorney (General, Medical, Durable)
  • Wills and Living Wills
  • Trust Documents
  • Affidavits and Sworn Statements
  • Medical Directives and Advance Directives
  • Parental Consent Forms
  • Name Change Documents

Real Estate

Purchase, Refinance & Closing Documents

Real estate transactions require precise, timely notarization. We work with buyers, sellers, lenders, title companies, and escrow officers to ensure every signing is completed accurately and on schedule.

  • Refinance Packages

What to Bring to Your Appointment

A little preparation keeps your signing quick and compliant.

🪪

Valid Photo ID

A current, government-issued photo ID such as a driver's license or passport. Must not be expired.

📄

Your Documents

Bring all documents to be notarized — complete but unsigned. Do not sign before the appointment.

👥

Required Witnesses

If your document requires witnesses, arrange them in advance. Ask us if you're unsure.
